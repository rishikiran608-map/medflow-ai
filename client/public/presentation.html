Slide 5 / 10 · How It Works

From Patient Triage to Pharmacy Dispatch

A simple 4-step pipeline that automates the entire clinic workflow.

📥
1. Patient Triage
Patient describes symptoms in English or Telugu. AI recommends specialist doctor.
🔮
2. Queue Tracking
Patient selects travel mode. AI calculates traffic ETA and updates wait times.
👨‍⚕️
3. Doctor Consultation
Doctor uses Gemini OCR & 1-click Auto-Draft SOAP to generate prescription notes.
💊
4. Pharmacy Dispatch
Prescription sent to pharmacy dashboard & WhatsApp ticket sent to patient.
Our platform automates the clinic workflow in 4 simple steps. Step 1 — Patient describes symptoms in natural language, AI recommends the right specialist doctor. Step 2 — Patient clicks "On My Way", AI calculates their commute ETA and live wait time. Step 3 — Doctor sees the live queue, uses Gemini Vision OCR and 1-click SOAP to complete the consultation in seconds. Step 4 — Digital prescription goes to pharmacy and WhatsApp confirmation goes to the patient.
