Slide 5 / 10 · How It Works

From Patient Triage to Pharmacy Dispatch

A simple 4-step pipeline that automates the entire clinic workflow.

📥
1. Patient Triage
Patient describes symptoms in English or Telugu. AI recommends specialist doctor.
🔮
2. Queue Tracking
Patient selects travel mode. AI calculates traffic ETA and updates wait times.
👨‍⚕️
3. Doctor Consultation
Doctor uses Gemini OCR & 1-click Auto-Draft SOAP to generate prescription notes.
💊
4. Pharmacy Dispatch
Prescription sent to pharmacy dashboard & WhatsApp ticket sent to patient.
